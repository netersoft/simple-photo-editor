Simple Photo Editor lets you edit your photos in a few taps and share them with your friends and family. Pick a photo from your gallery or take one with the camera, touch it up, add text, stickers or a drawing, then save and share it. Simple, free and ad-free.

★ FILTERS AND ADJUSTMENTS
• 16 filters: black & white, sepia, grayscale, lomo, dual tone, cross process, documentary, posterize, negative, auto fix…
• Brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth
• Crop, with preset ratios
• Rotate and mirror

★ CREATIVE TOOLS
• Text: write on your photos in the color of your choice
• Emojis and 35 stickers
• Brush: draw freely, with the color, size and opacity you want, and an eraser
• Move, enlarge and turn texts, emojis and stickers with your fingers
• Undo and redo every step

★ SAVE AND SHARE
• Your photo is saved in high definition (up to 2048 px) in the "Simple Photo Editor" album of your gallery
• Share it in one tap with the app of your choice
• Your collection: find your edited photos, view them full screen, edit them again, share or delete them
• Image info: file, size, dimensions and camera data (EXIF)

★ SIMPLE AND RESPECTFUL OF YOUR PRIVACY
• No ads, no account, no data collected: your photos stay on your phone
• Works without an internet connection
• Light and dark theme
• In English and French
• Free software (GNU GPL v3)
